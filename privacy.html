Wellvia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. Controller: Kazim ÖZGÜN (individual), kazim.ozgun87@icloud.com.

  • Data: birth year, sex, height, weight, goals, activity level, optional health flags, water and medication logs, daily step count read from Apple Health (read-only).
  • Storage: health data stays on your device. No account is created and nothing is sent to our servers. Notifications never show medication names.
  • AI Coach: runs entirely on your device in this version; nothing is sent to a server. If a cloud AI service is added later, only an anonymised summary (age band, goal, level) would be sent, with your separate consent and after this policy is updated.
  • Ads: the free tier has ad placements, but this version uses no third-party ad network or tracking and collects no data for advertising. If an ad partner is added later, it would require separate consent and an updated policy. Health data is never shared with ad partners.
  • Payments: handled by Apple App Store.
  • Your rights: access, correct, delete, restrict and withdraw consent. Use Profile in the app to export or delete all data, or email us.
  • Children: not directed at children under 13.
